# How the rewrite answers the 6/10 review of the original manuscript

| Criticism of the original | What changed |
|---|---|
| Thin novelty; no method or theory; the constant-estimate result is partly tautological | New method (conformal notice alarm) with a closed-form critical level and finite-sample notice guarantee (Lemma 2, Theorem 1), its training-conditional form (Corollary 1, checked against the spread of fold-level failure rates), a decision-aware choice that keeps it (Corollary 2), a certification floor 1/(n+1) (Proposition 1), a pathwise guarantee under arbitrary censoring (Theorem 2), a cross-fitted variant (Theorem 3), online calibration with a deterministic long-run bound for any sequence (Theorem 4), and uncertain lead times (Remark 1). The constant-estimate observation becomes Proposition 2, a bound that explains it, with an equivalence test (which did **not** confirm the earlier ±3.6-point claim; reported as a correction). |
| One narrow instrument (one GBM, one trigger, one window) | The method wraps any causal signal and monotone trigger; shown on a learned regressor and on a physics-based capacity trend, and, in a fifth hashed amendment, on three further learners (ridge, random forest, a neural network on raw sensor windows) and 27 alarm settings. The window is no longer the evaluation target; the failure-before-replacement probability is. |
| Mostly simulated data | Unchanged in substance (five of seven datasets are from one simulator family; 123 laboratory battery cells are the real data). An external laboratory dataset of the same cell type (HUST) was identified but could not be downloaded in the computing environment; listed as the first next step. |
| Heavy post hoc analysis | One main protocol hashed before the final experiments and four amendments, each hashed before its own experiment; hypotheses and decision rules fixed in advance; failed hypotheses reported (H3, H4, H7b); the one analysis added afterwards (training-conditional check) is labelled exploratory. |
| No equivalence test for the constant-estimate control | Tango score interval and TOST with a ±5-point margin fixed in the protocol (Table 7). |
| Weak transfer story | Leave-one-batch-out and leave-one-subset-out with a mechanism (exchangeability fails; the calibrated level has no margin), two remedies with guarantees (pilot recalibration: per-unit guarantee; online calibration: long-run bound for any sequence, no pilot failures), and an honest limit (short sequences). |
| Excessive length and density | About 18 pages in the RESS double-column template instead of 27, one headline contribution, supplementary material not needed. |
| Comparison with Kamariotis et al. only in an adapted setting | Added their decision grid and metric M; plus an equal-information comparison in which their policy and the cost-tuned alarms are rebuilt on the same five fold models as the cross-fitted certified alarm. |
